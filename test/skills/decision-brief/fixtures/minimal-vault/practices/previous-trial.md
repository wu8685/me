# Orchid Relay Queue Trial

The fictional team selected Route A after a two-day prototype. The prototype
tested throughput but not recovery after a worker lost its lease. During a later
staging exercise, duplicate work appeared and rollback took four hours.

The trial did not test Route B, so it cannot establish that Route B is better.
